Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during the Forge Assessment?

We spend 30–60 minutes reviewing your current workflows, identifying automation opportunities, and outlining a roadmap. It's a conversation, not a sales call. You'll walk away with clear next steps whether or not you choose to work with us.

Can I upgrade from Forge Launch to Guardian later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a Launch project to see results, then move to Guardian for ongoing management and continuous improvement. We'll credit your Launch investment toward Guardian setup.

What does the setup fee cover?

Discovery, solution design, initial deployment, staff onboarding, and testing. It's a one-time investment to ensure your AI systems are built correctly from day one.

Is there a minimum contract for Guardian?

Yes — 12 months. AI systems improve with data and iteration. A longer partnership ensures we can optimize, refine, and deliver compounding value over time.

What if I need something between tiers?

Every business is different. The tiers are starting points — we'll tailor a package that fits your specific needs, budget, and growth trajectory.

How do you handle the AWS billing for Managed Cloud?

You retain full ownership of your AWS account. We provide a monthly breakdown of all AWS costs plus our 10% management fee. No markup on infrastructure — complete transparency.

Do you support Azure or other cloud providers?

Yes. We're vendor-agnostic — AWS is simply where we start because it's the most widely adopted. If your business runs on Azure, Google Cloud, or a hybrid setup, we'll manage whatever stack you're on. Same 10% management model, same transparency. We recommend the right cloud for your workload, not the one we're most comfortable with.

We already have an AI chatbot. Why would we need Armory Forge Systems?

Most AI chatbots can answer questions. We focus on making AI genuinely useful. We optimize conversation flow, brand voice, customer experience, and automation so your AI doesn't just respond — it helps customers accomplish their goals.

Can you improve the AI we already have?

Yes. We can audit your current chatbot or voice agent, identify friction points, improve prompts and conversation design, and recommend automations that increase customer satisfaction and reduce workload.

How do you know if our AI needs improvement?

We perform an AI Conversation Audit. We review how your AI handles real customer interactions, identify missed opportunities, and provide recommendations to improve the experience.
