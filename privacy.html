Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Armory Forge Systems (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our services.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, company, email address, phone number, and the details of your inquiry. We may also collect limited technical information such as device, browser, and website-usage data.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our services, schedule or remind you about appointments, send requested service updates, and—where you have consented—send sales follow-ups and marketing text messages.

Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This includes mobile phone numbers, text-message opt-in data, and consent. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate our services or meet legal obligations, and not for their own marketing or promotional use.

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to four marketing messages per month; appointment, service, and inquiry-related messages may be sent when relevant. Message and data rates may apply. See our SMS Consent page and SMS Terms & Conditions.

How we protect and retain information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining records of SMS consent where applicable.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or email info@armoryforgesystems.com. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@armoryforgesystems.com.
