# Armory Forge Systems — Image Style Guide

> Lead concept artist brief for premium editorial artwork.

## Style

- Industrial futuristic
- Forged steel, black metal, brushed titanium
- Molten orange (#ff6b00) accent
- Glowing circuitry
- Premium cinematic lighting — Unreal Engine quality
- Hyper detailed, photorealistic, sharp focus, high contrast
- Corporate but fun — confident, optimistic
- Never dystopian, creepy, or horror

## Color Palette

Black · Gunmetal · Steel · Titanium · Orange forge glow · Small white accents

Every image should immediately feel like **Armory Forge Systems.**

## Branding (required)

- Article title in huge, bold industrial typography
- **Armory Forge Systems**
- *"You Imagine. We Build."*
- *"To the victor, the spoils."*
- AFS logo or AFS branding integrated naturally

## Visual Language

- Images tell the story before anyone reads the article
- Viewer understands the article within 3 seconds
- Always show the **problem** and the **solution**
- Expressive body language
- Robots always look helpful — smile with warmth, never creepy
- Robots are coworkers, not threats

## The Forge Bots

Recurring AFS characters. Every robot has:
- Black steel armor
- Orange glowing accents
- High-end engineering
- Friendly personality
- Visible mechanical details
- Premium industrial design

Think: **Boston Dynamics × Apple × Iron Man × Pacific Rim** — not Terminator.

## Humor & Easter Eggs

Every image should hide 2–5 easter eggs. Examples:

- Freedom Coffee
- TPS Reports
- Inbox Zero
- Ask Human First
- Definitely_Not_TPS_Reports
- Meeting that should've been an email
- CEO of Richest Company Ever
- World's Best Spreadsheet
- 404 Paperwork Not Found

Jokes reward people who zoom in. Never overdo it.

## Layout

- Leave breathing room around title
- Never hide important elements
- Compositions that work for marketing

## Deliverables per article

1. **Widescreen hero image** (article hero / card thumbnail)
2. **Square social image** (social sharing)

Both use identical visual language.

## Quality bar

Looks like a movie poster. Looks expensive. Looks handcrafted. Looks like an elite creative agency produced it. **Absolutely no generic AI art.**

## Article Image Workflow

For every new article, automatically generate:

1. **Hero image** (16:9 widescreen)
2. **Square social image** (1:1)
3. **Alt text** — descriptive, accessible, includes article topic
4. **SEO image filename** — descriptive, hyphenated (`small-business-automation-guide-hero.png`)
5. **Image caption** — short, attribution-ready
6. **Easter eggs used** — log which ones were embedded (keep variety across articles)
7. **Social media teaser** — 1–2 line hook for sharing

### Future Extensions

- **Carousels** — multi-image sets for Instagram posts (on demand)
- **Video** — animate hero images into short-form clips (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts)
