How Much Does an AI Receptionist Cost?

The Forge — Armory Forge Systems

Let's get straight to it: an AI receptionist costs anywhere from free to $3,000+ per month, depending on what you need.

But "how much does it cost" isn't really the question you're asking. You're asking: "Is it worth it for my business?"

Here's what the numbers actually look like.

The Range

Type Monthly Cost What You Get
DIY / White-label API $0–$200/mo Raw AI voice API. You build and manage everything yourself.
Off-the-shelf SaaS $200–$800/mo Pre-built voice agent, basic customization, limited integrations.
Custom-built agent $1,000–$3,000/mo Tailored to your business, full integration with your CRM/ calendar, brand voice, ongoing optimization.
Full-service + human backup $2,000–$5,000/mo AI handles first contact, humans handle escalations, white-glove setup and management.

The wide range exists because there's a huge difference between "an AI that answers the phone" and "an AI that represents your business well."

What You're Actually Paying For

A cheap AI receptionist answers calls and takes messages. That's a glorified voicemail with extra steps.

A good AI receptionist:

  • Understands your business well enough to answer real questions
  • Schedules appointments without back-and-forth
  • Captures lead information and pushes it to your CRM
  • Sounds professional and on-brand
  • Escalates intelligently when it should
  • Improves over time

That takes engineering, integration work, and ongoing tuning. That's what the higher end of the range covers.

The Other Side of the Equation

Scenario Cost
One missed consultation per week × 50 weeks $5,000–$25,000+ in lost revenue
Employee time spent screening and transferring calls $800–$2,000/mo of buried payroll
Voicemails that never get returned Hard to quantify, but it's not zero

A single booked consultation from an AI-handled call can cover the monthly cost.

The Break-Even Math

If you're a small business taking 20–50 inbound calls per month:

  • Without AI: Most after-hours calls go to voicemail. Some get returned. Some don't.
  • With AI: Every call is answered. Information is captured. Consultations are scheduled.

If even one extra client per quarter comes from calls that would have otherwise been missed, the AI pays for itself.

What to Look For

When evaluating AI receptionist options, ask:

  1. Is it tailored to my business? A generic agent sounds generic.
  2. Does it integrate with my calendar and CRM? If not, you're still doing manual work.
  3. Can it handle industry-specific questions? A real estate agent needs different answers than a plumber.
  4. What's the escalation process? How and when does a human get involved?
  5. Who tunes and improves it? AI isn't set-it-and-forget-it.
