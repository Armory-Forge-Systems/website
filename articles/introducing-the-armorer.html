Introducing The Armorer

The Forge — Armory Forge Systems

Every forge needs a front door. Someone to greet the client, understand what they need, and make sure the right craftsperson takes the job.

At Armory Forge Systems, that front door is The Armorer.

What Is The Armorer?

The Armorer is our first flagship AI agent — the primary interface between AFS and the people who need us. It handles two critical channels:

📞 Voice. Inbound calls are answered by an agent that listens, understands, and responds with the same precision a master craftsperson brings to the forge. It answers questions, gathers requirements, and schedules consultations — or escalates to a human when the job demands it.

💬 Web. The Armorer lives on our website as an intelligent guide, helping visitors understand our services, qualify their needs, and take the next step without friction.

Why "The Armorer"?

The Mandalorian Armorer forges the highest-quality beskar in the galaxy. Silent. Precise. Every word earned. Every action deliberate.

That's the energy we wanted for this agent. Not a generic chatbot. Not a voice menu tree. A professional, always-available presence that reflects the craftsmanship values of AFS.

Every interaction reinforces a simple message:

You imagine. We build. To the winner, the spoils.

The Tech Behind It

The Armorer runs on Hermes — our orchestration platform. Think of Hermes as the workshop: the tools, the workflows, the backend logic. The Armorer is the craftsperson at the counter, using those tools to serve clients.

This separation matters. Hermes can coordinate any number of specialized agents — future ones will handle project management, support, operations, and more. The Armorer is the first, not the last.

What This Means for Your Business

If you've ever called a business after hours and gotten a voicemail that never got returned, you know exactly why The Armorer exists.

If you've ever visited a website and couldn't find the answer to a simple question without filling out a contact form, you know why we built this.

The Armorer doesn't replace people. It removes the friction between a potential client and the help they need. It answers when you can't. It guides when they're unsure. It books a conversation when it's time to talk to a human.

That's the entire point: serve the client well, every time, from the first interaction.

Want to Meet It?

The forge is open. The Armorer is ready.
