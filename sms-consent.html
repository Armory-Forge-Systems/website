SMS Consent & Opt-In

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Armory Forge Systems sends text messages only to people who have given express consent through our website or who separately initiate a text-message opt-in. We use messages for appointment reminders, sales follow-ups related to an inquiry, service and account updates, and occasional marketing.

How website opt-in works

Visitors may provide a mobile number on The Armorer support-intake form at armorer.html. SMS consent is optional and is collected through a separate, unchecked checkbox. A visitor can submit the inquiry form without agreeing to receive SMS messages.

“I agree to receive recurring SMS messages from Armory Forge Systems about my inquiry, appointments, services, and occasional marketing. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies (up to 4 marketing messages per month); message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Terms and Privacy Policy.”

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies according to your interaction with us. You may receive up to four marketing messages per month. Appointment reminders, service updates, and direct sales follow-ups are sent only when relevant to your request, appointment, or business relationship. Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out of SMS messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email info@armoryforgesystems.com.
  • Consent is not a condition of buying any goods or services.

Proof of consent

For each website opt-in, we maintain a record of the mobile number, opt-in date and time, source form, consent status, and the consent-language version presented to the visitor.
